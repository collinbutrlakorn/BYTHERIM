Everyone

The official universe

BYTHERIM runs the official season and publishes it to the site. Everyone sees the same results: the draft board and draft night on the Draft RP, the transfer portal and college careers on Recruiting, and the current week on the RP Hub.

It moves forward each time a new week is published.

You

Your own save

Press New Save in the NCAA Sim to start a universe of your own from the same rosters and recruits. Your save never changes the official one.

It lives in this browser on this device. Use Export Save in the sim's menu to back it up or move it to another device, and Import Save to bring it back.

A season, start to finish

The season plays out in the NCAA RP and the draft in the Draft RP; the other pages follow along.

  1. 1

    Regular season

    Simulate a week at a time, or watch any game live with play-by-play, a box score and a win-probability chart. Standings, the Top 25, award races and every player's stat line update as you go, and the Draft RP's big board moves with every game.

  2. 2

    Postseason

    Seeded conference tournaments, Selection Sunday, then the NCAA Tournament, one round at a time, with every game watchable live. Each conference tournament and region names a Most Outstanding Player, and the Final Four names its own. Big performances in big games move draft stock, both ways.

  3. 3

    The draft cycle, in the Draft RP

    Once a champion is crowned, players declare. In the Draft RP you run the combine (measurements, athletic testing, shooting, interviews), the lottery, team workouts and the withdrawal deadline. Every step moves a prospect's stock. The NCAA RP waits for you.

  4. 4

    Draft night

    Sixty picks, revealed one at a time. Teams lean toward players who impressed them in workouts. The pick stays on his college record, and every past draft and final big board stays in the Draft RP.

  5. 5

    Transfer portal and new rosters

    Back in the NCAA RP: players transfer, the next recruiting class enrolls, rosters are set, and the new season tips off.

Your first season, step by step

New here? This is everything you need for your first save. Prefer to be shown? Take a quick tour of the NCAA RP, Recruiting, the Draft RP or the RP Hub.

  1. 1

    Start a save

    Open the NCAA RP and press Start a new save. It builds the universe from the official rosters and recruits, plays the summer before the season, and drops you at the preseason. It's saved in your browser automatically; sign in with Google (top right) to also keep it in your account and play on another device.

  2. 2

    Play the regular season

    Press Simulate Week to play a week. Skip ahead jumps to conference play, the conference tournaments, the NCAA Tournament or the offseason. On the Dashboard, Watch any upcoming game live, or click any score for its box score (starters are marked) and play-by-play.

  3. 3

    Look around

    Teams has every program: roster, stats, schedule, and Team History, where any past season opens as its own team page. Team Stats and Player Stats are the national leaderboards, each with a search box. Standings, Bracketology, Awards, Recruits, History and the Record Book follow the season. Click any player for his page; the school in each of his season rows opens that year's team.

  4. 4

    March

    Conference tournaments are seeded from the standings. Selection Sunday reveals the 68-team field, then the tournament plays one round at a time. Big games move draft stock.

  5. 5

    The offseason

    After the title game the NCAA RP walks you through the offseason, one step at a time: the season wrap-up, the NBA draft (run it in the Draft RP: combine, lottery, workouts, withdrawal deadline, draft night), the transfer portal, the summer circuit (AAU and the FIBA youth World Cup, playable on the Recruiting page) and final rosters. Along the way players develop and the coaching carousel turns. Then the next season tips off.

  6. 6

    Keep it safe

    Your save stays in this browser until you clear your browsing data. From the … menu you can Export a copy, Import it later, or save it to your account.

How the simulation decides things

For the curious: what's under the hood.

Ratings and development

Every player has an overall rating. Young players with room to grow and real minutes improve the most each offseason; seniors and bench players change less. A recruit's high-school, AAU and FIBA numbers shape how he plays in college: his shooting, his free throws, how often he takes threes, how much he passes.

Who starts and who plays

Coaches start their best five, keeping two or three guards and at least one big. A player listed at two positions (like a PF/C) can cover the other spot when the lineup needs it. Top freshmen get an early leash; after a dozen games, form decides who keeps his job, and a blue-chip freshman who loses his often transfers.

Recruiting

The two classes still in high school are recruited as your seasons play. Lists shrink from a dozen schools to a top 8, a top 5 and a final 3. Programs pull by prestige, how they're trending, last March, their coach and teammates already there; a coaching change can reopen a commitment. Commitments written on the official sheet never move. Overseas prospects can come to college or stay pro.

The draft board

Prospects are scored on production, efficiency, age, size, athleticism and pedigree, and big games move them. International pros are judged on upside, some have breakout seasons, and a thin college class leans on them more. Pros enter and withdraw like college underclassmen, and once in a while one who withdraws leaves his club for a college.

BPM

Box Plus/Minus is figured the way Barttorvik does it for college players, not Basketball-Reference's NBA version: college weights for each box-score stat, then a team adjustment against the team's opponent-adjusted efficiency margin. A great line on a strong team in a hard league rates higher than the same line in a soft one, and big men tend to rate higher than guards, as they do on Barttorvik.

Prestige and coaches

A program's prestige follows its history, its recent seasons and its coach, and decides the level of recruit it can sign. Coaches get fired and hired every spring, and some players follow their coach.

Stars, ratings and ranks

Recruits are rated out of 100: 90+ is a five-star, 80+ four stars, 70+ three. The national rank follows the ratings. RSCI is a recruit's national rank coming out of high school.

Questions

Are the players real?

No. Every player and recruit in BYTHERIM RP is fictional, made up for the RP. If a name matches a real person, that's a coincidence. The schools and conferences are real; the players are not.

Why doesn't my draft match the official one?

Each save plays out on its own. Some picks are set in advance by BYTHERIM and land in the same slot in every save, but the rest depends on how your seasons go.

Will my save disappear?

Not on its own, but it's stored in your browser. Clearing your browsing data, or using a private window, removes it. Export your save now and then to keep a copy.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Everything works on a phone; simulating whole seasons is quicker on a computer.

How often does the official universe move forward?

Whenever BYTHERIM publishes new results. The RP Hub always shows the current week and where the draft stands.
