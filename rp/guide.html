How the RP works

One college basketball universe across three pages: players are recruited out of high school, play their college careers in the simulation, and leave through the NBA draft.

Two ways to follow along

Watch the official universe, run your own, or both.

Everyone

The official universe

BYTHERIM runs the official season and publishes it to the site. Everyone sees the same results: the draft board and draft night on the Draft RP, the transfer portal and college careers on Recruiting, and the current week on the RP Hub.

It moves forward each time a new week is published.

You

Your own save

Press New Save in the NCAA Sim to start a universe of your own from the same rosters and recruits. Your save never changes the official one.

It lives in this browser on this device. Use Export Save in the sim's menu to back it up or move it to another device, and Import Save to bring it back.

A season, start to finish

Everything below happens in the NCAA Sim; the other pages follow along.

  1. 1

    Regular season

    Simulate a week at a time. Standings, the Top 25, award races and every player's stat line update as you go, and the in-season big board moves with every game.

  2. 2

    Postseason

    Conference tournaments, then the NCAA Tournament, one round at a time.

  3. 3

    Declarations and the combine

    Once a champion is crowned, players declare for the draft. Combine and workouts move their stock, and some decide to return to school.

  4. 4

    Draft night

    The NBA draft is part of every offseason: a lottery, sixty picks, and a team for every drafted player. The pick stays on his college record.

  5. 5

    Transfer portal and new rosters

    Players transfer, the next recruiting class enrolls, rosters are set, and the new season begins.

The three pages

01 · Recruiting

From signing day on

Class and school rankings, prospect profiles and commitments. The Transfer Portal tab lists every school change: Scheduled moves written into the BYTHERIM rosters, Portal moves the simulation made, and Upcoming ones that take effect next season. Once a recruit plays, his profile shows his college seasons and draft pick.

 02 · NCAA Sim

Where the season is played

Every Division I program: schedules and results, team and player pages, standings, the bracket and the archive of past seasons. Start a new save or continue your own from its home screen.

 03 · Draft RP

The road to the pros

All season: a big board and mock draft built from every college player. After the tournament: the declared class. After draft night: the real picks and teams, with past drafts a click away. Build My Big Board from any prospect; it's saved in your browser. When you have your own save too, switch between Official and My Save at the top.

Questions

Why doesn't my draft match the official one?

Each save plays out on its own. Some picks are set in advance by BYTHERIM and land in the same slot in every save, but the rest depends on how your seasons go.

Will my save disappear?

Not on its own, but it's stored in your browser. Clearing your browsing data, or using a private window, removes it. Export your save now and then to keep a copy.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Everything works on a phone; simulating whole seasons is quicker on a computer.

How often does the official universe move forward?

Whenever BYTHERIM publishes new results. The RP Hub always shows the current week and where the draft stands.
