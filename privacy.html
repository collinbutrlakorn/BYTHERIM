Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026 · Updated November 13, 2026

BYTHERIM (bytherim.com) is an independent basketball site run by Collin Butrlakorn. This page explains what information the site collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: you can use almost all of BYTHERIM without an account, we don't sell anything about you, and we don't run ads.

What we collect

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional. It's only used to save your BYTHERIM RP progress and draft boards to your account so they follow you across devices. When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo with us through Firebase Authentication, a Google service. BYTHERIM's own database stores only:

  • an account ID (a random identifier from Firebase);
  • your saved NCAA RP universe and your draft boards.

Your name, email address and photo stay with Firebase Authentication, which the site uses to show you who's signed in. Earlier versions of the site also copied your name and email into the database; nothing used them, so they're no longer saved, and any old copies are cleared the next time you sign in. Site administrators are listed by email address so the site knows who may publish official content.

Usage statistics

To understand how the site is used, BYTHERIM counts page views and a handful of actions (for example, starting a new RP save or simulating a week). These are stored only as daily totals, such as "120 views of the Draft page today." No name, email, IP address or account ID is attached to them. To count visitors without double-counting, your browser remembers on your own device whether it has already been counted that day; that note never leaves your browser. If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is counted.

Ratings and feedback

The "Rate the sim" link on the RP pages sends a star rating from 1 to 5 and an optional note of up to 500 characters, along with the page you were on and the date. It isn't tied to your account, name, email address or IP address, and only the site's administrator can read it. Please don't put personal details in the note. Ratings and notes aren't published.

Cookies and local storage

BYTHERIM doesn't set cookies of its own, and it doesn't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Instead the site uses your browser's local storage to remember things you'd expect it to: your light/dark theme and settings, your RP saves (which live in your browser unless you sign in and save them to your account), that you've already been counted today, which social embeds you've chosen to load, that you've seen the sign-in notice, and, for administrators, that you're signed in as one. Signing in with Google also uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in. This information stays on your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

The only cookies you may meet come from other companies, and only if you choose to load their content (see below) or to sign in with Google. Because nothing like that starts until you ask, the site doesn't show a cookie banner.

Server logs

BYTHERIM is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log your IP address and browser details when you load a page, for security and operations. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Content from other services

Some pages show content loaded from other companies, which may set cookies and collect information under their own privacy policies when that content loads:

  • YouTube videos load (in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode) only after you press play;
  • Posts and feeds from X, Instagram and TikTok load only after you press "Load" on that card. You can ask the site to remember your choice on this device, and undo it here:
  • Google: Firebase (sign-in and saves), Google Sheets (where the site's rankings and rosters are kept) and Google Fonts, which your browser requests from Google whenever a page loads, so Google sees your IP address;
  • rss2json, which reads the public Substack and podcast feeds for the post and episode lists;
  • public code libraries served from jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs.

Links to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, Substack and other sites take you to those services, and they aren't covered by this policy.

How we use it

Only to run the site: keeping you signed in, storing and restoring your saves and boards, letting administrators publish content, and seeing which parts of the site people use so we can improve them. We don't sell or rent personal information, don't use it for advertising, and don't share it except with the service providers above that host and run the site, or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your information

Account data stays until you ask us to delete it. To have your account, saves and boards deleted, contact us (below) from the email address you signed in with, and we'll remove them. You can sign out at any time from the account menu, and you can clear the site's data from your browser settings.

Children

BYTHERIM is a general-audience basketball site and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to how it's used. Contact us to make a request. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising, and we treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out of usage counting.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact
