Accessibility

Updated November 13, 2026

BYTHERIM should work for everyone who loves basketball, including people who use a keyboard instead of a mouse, a screen reader, a magnifier or a high-contrast display. We're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 level AA, and we check pages with automated tools and keyboard-only navigation. We haven't had the site formally audited, and we're still fixing what we find.

What's in place

  • A "Skip to main content" link at the top of every page, which appears when you press Tab.
  • Menus, buttons and forms that can be used from the keyboard, with a visible focus outline. The sign-in and rating dialogs close with Escape and return you to where you were.
  • Text and controls colored to meet AA contrast in both the dark and light themes.
  • Descriptions for images that carry meaning. Decorative images, such as team logos shown beside team names, are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Labels on buttons, filters and form fields, and a layout that adapts from phones to desktops without blocking zoom.
  • Social posts from X, Instagram and TikTok that load only when you ask, so nothing moves or autoplays on its own.

Known limits

  • The RP simulation pages are dense, with large tables and a live game view. Some clickable parts of them, such as table rows and team logos that open a profile, can't be reached with the keyboard yet, and we're still improving how they work with screen readers.
  • Social posts and videos come from YouTube, X, Instagram and TikTok, and follow those services' own accessibility.

Tell us about a problem

If something on BYTHERIM is hard or impossible for you to use, tell us what page, what you were trying to do and what device or assistive technology you use: reach out by direct message on Instagram or X. We'll try to fix it, or get you the information another way.
