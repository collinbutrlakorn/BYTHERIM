About BYTHERIM

Welcome to BYTHERIM Recruiting—your premier, independent destination for comprehensive fictional basketball prospect analysis, class rankings, and in-depth player statistics. This platform was built by Collin (@collinbutr on X/twitter) out of a lifelong passion for the game, detailed tracking, and a desire to bring professional-grade metrics and organization to amateur basketball evaluation.

Whether you're tracking elite high school standouts, analyzing advanced shooting profiles, or following school recruiting classes, BYTHERIM provides granular data to give you a complete picture of tomorrow's stars.
