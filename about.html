BEHIND THE PLATFORM

THE BYTHERIM ETHOS

Constructed for basketball purists, draft evaluators, and fans who value deep film study over surface-level box scores and media narratives.

PLATFORM PILLARS

01 / INDEPENDENCE

Unbiased Coverage

BYTHERIM operates with total editorial autonomy. Free from main-stream media narratives, sponsor influence, and sensationalist commentary, every evaluation is anchored strictly in game tape and verifiable metrics.

02 / SCOUTING

Context-First Scouting

We break down physical mechanics, spatial geometry, defensive rotations, and pick-and-roll reads. Traditional box scores reveal outcomes—our breakdowns explain the tactical choices that drive them.

03 / SIMULATION & TECH

The Recruiting Portal

Beyond traditional media, BYTHERIM houses an interactive role-playing database engine. The RP system tracks prospect ratings, offer sheets, and recruiting class standings in real time.

THE EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

FILM STUDY & GEOMETRY

Every report starts with micro-level tape evaluation. We track dynamic off-ball movement, rim deterrence angles, ball-handler deceleration, and physical leverage at the point of attack.

ADVANCED METRIC INTEGRATION

Film insights are paired with impact metrics, usage context, shooting gravity, and historical translation baselines to separate inflated production from real NBA-caliber skills.

Fuel Independent Scouting

BYTHERIM is self-funded and operated independently. Contributions directly support server compute for the Recruiting Portal database, live scouting event operations, and video editing tools.
