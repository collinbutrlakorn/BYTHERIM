Behind the platform

The BYTHERIM ethos

Built for basketball purists, draft evaluators, and fans who value deep analysis over surface-level box scores and media narratives.

Platform pillars

01 · Independence

Unbiased coverage

BYTHERIM operates with total editorial autonomy. Free from mainstream media narratives, sponsor influence and sensationalist commentary, every evaluation is anchored in game tape and verifiable metrics.

02 · Scouting

Context-first scouting

We break down physical mechanics, spacing, defensive rotations and pick-and-roll reads. Box scores reveal outcomes — our breakdowns explain the choices that drive them.

03 · Simulation

The RP universe

Beyond traditional media, BYTHERIM runs an interactive college basketball universe: a full Division I simulation, a recruiting database, and a draft of its own, season after season.

The evaluation framework

Film

Film study

Every report starts with tape. Off-ball movement, rim deterrence, how a ball-handler changes speed, and physical leverage at the point of attack.

Data

Advanced metrics

Film is paired with impact metrics, usage context, shooting gravity and historical translation baselines, to separate inflated production from real NBA-caliber skill.
